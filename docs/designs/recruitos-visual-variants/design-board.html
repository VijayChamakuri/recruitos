Design shotgun: RecruitOS application variants

2026-09-05 · inside the APPROVED direction in DESIGN.md · paper and graphite · nothing in the locked list is up for a vote

All three variants use the same tokens, the same three type systems, the same four rationed hues, and the same closed component inventory. They differ on the one thing DESIGN.md deliberately leaves open: how the packet's three surfaces are arranged and where the command center lives. The rest of the application is built once, in the recommended structure, and is linked below the scorecard.

ALedgerscore 21/30
variant-a.html · 31.5%

One continuous ledger. Arithmetic, evidence, and source sit at nearly the same surface value, separated only by hairlines. Evidence renders as flat rows, not cards. The queue is the command center, with a single hairline run strip and no readouts above it.

Where it wins
  • Highest density of the three. Most rows and most evidence per viewport.
  • Most obviously not a dashboard. Reads as an instrument log.
  • Cheapest to build. Almost no pane chrome to maintain.
Where it loses
  • The paper claim collapses. If the source pane is the same value as the chrome, "evidence should read as paper" stops being visible.
  • The three type systems do most of the separation work alone, which is a lot of load on typography that is not yet self-hosted.
  • No home for the command center readouts, so the founder and talent-lead flows have nowhere to land.
BBenchrecommended · 27/30
variant-b.html · 31.5%

Three panes at three declared surface values: graphite arithmetic, neutral evidence ledger, paper source. The evidence bracket is promoted from a left rule into a thread that draws a visible dashed line through all three panes when a claim is focused. The command center is an instrument band of readouts above the queue, hairline-separated, no cards and no gauges.

Where it wins
  • Surface value carries the paper-and-graphite claim without spending any hue.
  • The thread makes the signature component something you can see in a still screenshot, which is what capture 02 needs.
  • The band gives the command center a home without adding a sixth destination to the locked IA.
  • Injection flag, refused span, and gap card all have room to render at full fidelity.
Where it loses
  • The thread is the most implementation work in the set. It needs measured positions, and it must be disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • The band is one scroll of vertical budget the recruiter's daily loop does not need.
  • Three surface values is one more contrast pair to verify in dark mode.
CDossierscore 19/30
variant-c.html · 31.5%

Document-first. The source document takes the widest pane, evidence becomes margin annotations aligned to the spans they explain, and the arithmetic narrows to a 250px sticky ledger. The command center is a serif run report page with the queue below it.

Where it wins
  • Best pure reading experience. This is what "no ATS renders a resume as a document" was arguing for.
  • Marginalia is a genuinely legible metaphor for grounded evidence.
  • The run report is the most quotable single screen for a non-technical reader.
Where it loses
  • It quietly demotes the arithmetic. DESIGN.md says arithmetic and evidence are equally first-class, and a 250px column against a full-width document is not equal.
  • Three permanent slots per dimension do not survive a margin. Empty supporting and contradicting slots get collapsed into a footnote, which is the exact silent-absence failure Part 8 rule 5 bans.
  • The report page puts a scroll between the recruiter and the daily loop.

Recommendation: B, Bench

B is the only variant that breaks no locked constraint. A loses the paper claim, which is the reason light mode is primary at all. C is the most pleasant to read and it breaks two rules to get there: it demotes the arithmetic below the evidence, and it cannot hold three permanent slots per dimension, which is the exact silent-absence failure Part 8 rule 5 exists to make impossible.
Take two things from the losers. From A, the density discipline: the queue keeps its plain table and the instrument band never grows into cards. From C, the document typography: the source pane keeps its 68ch measure and its section heads, because C proved the reading experience is worth protecting.
The one thing to watch in B is the thread. It is the best part of the design and the easiest part to ship badly. If the measured positions turn out to be fragile across resize, virtualization, or reduced motion, cut the connector line and keep the synchronized bracket highlight. The claim survives. The connector is the flourish on top of it.

Known limitation of this board, on the record
IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Sans Condensed, Source Serif 4, and IBM Plex Mono are not installed on this machine and these mockups do not bundle them, so every frame above renders in fallback faces. Structure, spacing, and color are accurate. Typographic texture is not. That is the same failure Part 17 note 1 exists to prevent, showing up here first.
